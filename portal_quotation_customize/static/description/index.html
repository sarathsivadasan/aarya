Odoo Portal Quotation Editor | Edit Quantity & Delete Sale Order Lines

Odoo Portal Quotation Editor allows customers and portal users to modify sales quotations directly from the customer portal. Portal users can update product quantities and remove quotation lines before confirming an order, giving them more flexibility during the quotation review process.
The module updates the related backend sale order automatically, so sales teams always see the latest customer changes. Quantity changes recalculate the quotation amount, and deleted lines are removed from the quotation after confirmation.
Editing is available only for draft and sent quotations, helping businesses keep confirmed sale orders protected. The module also validates portal access and ensures that users can only update lines that belong to the correct quotation.

This app is useful for B2B sales, wholesale portals, distributors, service businesses, and any company that wants customers to adjust quotation requirements online without repeatedly contacting the sales team.

Features

Portal Quantity Editing

Customers can update product quantities directly from the Odoo quotation portal page. Once the quantity is changed, the backend sale order is updated automatically.

Delete Quotation Lines

Portal users can remove unwanted products or services from a quotation before confirming the order. A confirmation popup helps avoid accidental deletion.

Backend Sale Order Sync

Every portal-side change is reflected in the related sale order, keeping customer-facing and backend quotation data aligned.

Draft Quotation Protection

Portal editing is allowed only while the quotation is in draft or sent state. Confirmed orders stay protected from customer-side modification.

Secure Portal Access

The module checks quotation access before allowing updates and verifies that the selected line belongs to the correct quotation.

Better Customer Self-Service

Customers can adjust quotation requirements online without waiting for manual updates from the sales team.

What is the purpose of the Portal Quotation Customize module?

The Portal Quotation Customize module allows portal users to edit product quantities and delete order lines directly within a quotation. These changes are automatically reflected in the backend sale order, streamlining the quotation and sales order process.

What can users edit in a quotation?

Users can edit product quantities on quotation lines, allowing them to adjust the number of items in their order. They can also delete entire order lines from the quotation if they no longer wish to purchase specific products or services.

Who can edit or delete quotation lines?

Any user can edit quotation product quantities and delete lines.

How are changes made in the portal reflected in the backend?

Any changes made to the quotation, such as editing quantities or deleting lines, are automatically synchronized with the backend sale order in real time. This ensures consistency information in the system, eliminating the need for manual updates.

Can users modify the price or apply discounts on the quotation?

While the module primarily focuses on modifying quantities and deleting lines, pricing and discounts are typically governed by pre-set rules and business logic in the system. If the system is configured to allow it, prices may adjust automatically based on the quantity changes or specific discounts.
