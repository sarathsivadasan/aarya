UAE VAT-201 REPORT

This module allows printing/exporting UAE VAT-201 Reports in Odoo.

Overview

This Report module enhances the capabilities of the Odoo Accounting module within the framework of UAE VAT 201 compliance. This module facilitates the creation of comprehensive tax reports in Excel format, covering a specified period from a defined start date to an end date. Users can customize the report generation based on various criteria through additional options and fields available in the wizard interface. Furthermore, the report provides detailed insights by presenting both the tax amounts and the corresponding base amounts for sales and purchases. Ultimately, this functionality streamlines the process of obtaining consolidated tax reports, encompassing both purchases and sales transactions over the designated timeframe.

Features

Tax Report Generation: UAE VAT 201 facilitates the generation of detailed tax reports for businesses operating within the UAE VAT framework. This includes the ability to generate reports in Excel format, covering specific time periods and offering customizable criteria for analysis. These reports are essential for compliance and provide businesses with insights into their VAT obligations.
Comprehensive Analysis: UAE VAT 201 offers enhanced analysis capabilities, allowing businesses to delve into the details of their tax liabilities. Users can access breakdowns of tax amounts and base amounts for both sales and purchases, providing a comprehensive understanding of their VAT transactions. This feature aids businesses in managing their VAT obligations effectively and ensures compliance with UAE tax regulations.
