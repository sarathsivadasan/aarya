Invoice Report Enhance

Professional automotive workshop Tax Invoice for the ODEX WMS

Enhances the standard Odoo invoice report without replacing it. The layout is redesigned through pure QWeb / XPath inheritance of account.report_invoice_document, so the standard report engine, header (logo & TRN) and footer remain untouched and every Odoo upgrade stays safe.

  • Keeps the standard curved Odoo header and footer.
  • Invoice info row: Invoice Date, Due Date, Salesperson, Payment Terms.
  • Two-column Customer & Vehicle detail cards (empty fields hidden).
  • Separate Parts and Labour tables with per-line tax amounts.
  • Totals summary, amount in words, customer acknowledgement block.
  • Dynamic QR code linking to the customer portal invoice page.
  • Multi-company, multi-language, PDF & HTML preview compatible.
