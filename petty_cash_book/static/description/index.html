Petty Cash Book

A complete Petty Cash Management System for Odoo 18 Community Edition.

Features

✅ Dashboard with Opening Balance, Total Received, Total Expenses, Closing Balance & Cash in Hand
✅ Date range filters: Today, This Week, This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, This Year
✅ Cash Received (Income) tracking with auto journal entries
✅ Expense (Spending) tracking with auto journal entries
✅ Sequences: PCR/YYYY/001 and PCE/YYYY/001
✅ Professional QWeb PDF Reports: Petty Cash Book, Receipt Voucher, Expense Voucher
✅ Multi-company support
✅ Security groups: User and Manager
✅ Expense Categories with account mapping
✅ Chatter & activity tracking
